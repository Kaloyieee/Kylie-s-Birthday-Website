✨ For Your 20th Birthday ✨

You Are Never Alone

Twenty years of carrying quiet battles,
Of smiling through days when your chest felt heavy.
I’ve seen how you stood back up when no one was clapping,
How you mended yourself even when dreams felt fragile and unsteady.
And through every tired thought and silent doubt,
I want this to sink into your heart: you are never alone.

You don’t have to hold the weight of the whole world by yourself,
You don’t have to be strong every single second, love.
On the quiet nights when it feels like nobody understands,
There is one heart watching gently, listening closely, choosing you every day.
Look at all the voices that came right before this—
People who love you not for perfection, but simply for being who you are.

As you step into this new decade of your life,
I’m holding your hand—right beside you, right behind you.
When your strength runs low, I’ll catch you;
When the road gets blurry, we’ll find your way together.
Happy 20th birthday, my love, Kylie.
I love you so deeply, and you will never be alone. 🤍

— Always right here for you 💌
